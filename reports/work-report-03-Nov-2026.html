Website redesign: market research and two design prototypes

NameTalha
DateTuesday, 3 November 2026
Hours7 hours

1. Project summary

I researched leading websites to see what makes their design stronger and where ours falls short, then built two design prototypes from that research. Both use the same client content, so only design and layout change. The current live design is untouched.

2. UI analysis 2.5 hours

Reviewed the reference sites shared (including brevy.com and mavenclinic.com). They win with large, confident typography, a restrained palette with one accent, deliberate spacing, framed photography and purposeful motion. Our current design reads as flat: sections look alike, hierarchy is weak and buttons give little feedback. After building the prototypes, I reviewed them in the browser at different window sizes, tightened the gap between the nav bar and the hero, resized the hero photo to fit the screen height and re-centered the subject. Type checks pass.

3. Prototype 1: Second page 2 hours

Route /second. Medical-style look with a navy and cobalt palette, light display headlines with italic accents, a hero with a framed photo and glass detail cards, scroll reveals, and clear hover and press feedback on buttons.

4. Prototype 2: Third page 2.5 hours

Route /third. A new layout and identity in pine green and coral: arched hero photo with a call button, sticky step cards, a scrolling services rail, tabbed benefits, interactive stories, FAQ accordion and lead form. Motion includes a full-screen menu that opens from the toggle button, sliding button fills, a tab color wipe and scroll reveals, with reduced-motion support.
